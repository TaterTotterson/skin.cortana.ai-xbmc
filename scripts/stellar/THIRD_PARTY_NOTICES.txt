stellar-attach / attach.xbe
===========================

This skin includes an unmodified binary copy of attach.xbe from the
MakeMHz stellar-attach project.

Project: https://github.com/MakeMHz/stellar-attach
Bundled release: v1.1.0
Bundled asset: https://github.com/MakeMHz/stellar-attach/releases/download/v1.1.0/attach.xbe
License: GNU General Public License v2.0
License text: stellar-attach-LICENSE-GPL-2.0.txt
SHA-256: d7f01cdc64ab1469800fc57ffef9b34bb0451cf1f31423e73eca04d173eec801

attach.xbe is used as a separate helper executable for Project Stellar NetISO
mounting. The Cortana AI skin writes attach.ini beside the helper and launches
attach.xbe through XBMC.
